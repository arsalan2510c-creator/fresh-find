New Sabzi Mandi

Karachi's largest wholesale-to-retail produce hub, buzzing from dawn.

★★★★☆4.3 · 474 reviews
About the market

Where Super Highway comes for what's fresh

Trucks from Sindh, Punjab and Balochistan roll in before sunrise, making this the city's primary wholesale hub. Retail shoppers who arrive early get first pick at unbeatable prices.

Stalls open early and stay stocked through the day, with produce arriving from nearby farms and wholesale routes. Expect friendly bargaining, seasonal variety, and prices that reward a little walking between stalls.
